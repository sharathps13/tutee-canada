      07 Scholarships & support

      Funding isa strategy,not luck.

      Most international students combine several sources. Here's how each one works — and what decides eligibility.

      We don't publish award amounts: they change every intake. A counsellor matches awards to your profile.

      1. Many Canadian universities offer entrance scholarships to international students, usually based on academic results and sometimes on leadership or a separate application.

        • Competitive and limited in number
        • Criteria, amounts and deadlines are set by each institution, every year
        • Some are automatic on admission; others need a separate application

      2. The Government of Canada lists scholarships open to international students on its official portal. Most target specific countries, levels of study or research areas.

        EduCanada — Scholarships ↗

      3. Research master's and doctoral students may be funded through teaching or research assistantships, or a supervisor's grant. This depends entirely on the programme and department.

        • Contact potential supervisors early
        • Funding offers are made by the department, not guaranteed by admission

      4. We help you prepare the documents lenders ask for and understand collateral, moratorium periods and disbursement — and how a sanction letter can support proof of funds.

        Eligibility, rates and Canada coverage are decided by each lender, case by case.

        • Academic record and test scores
        • Level and programme of study — many awards are level-specific
        • Citizenship or country of residence
        • Deadlines, often months before the admission deadline
        • Funds you can show for the study permit — scholarships count only when confirmed

        09b Coming with family Important

        Bringing your family?Know this first.

        Family plans change which programme you should choose. These rules decide whether your spouse can work and how much money you must show — so we check them before you apply, not after.

        01

        Your spouse or partner

        An open work permit for a student's spouse or common-law partner is only available for certain programmes — mainly master's programmes of at least 16 months, doctoral programmes and some professional degree programmes (for example medicine, law, nursing or education).

        Spouses of most college diploma and bachelor's students cannot get this permit. They can still apply to visit, or for their own study or work permit.

        02

        Your children

        Minor children who come with a parent who is authorised to study in Canada can usually attend pre-school, primary or secondary school without needing their own study permit. Each child still needs the right visitor document to enter.

        Dependent children don't get open work permits through your study permit.

        03

        Money for everyone

        Your proof of funds must cover tuition plus living costs for every family member coming with you. For applications from 1 September 2026 the minimum for one person outside Québec is C$23,448 a year; IRCC adds an amount for each extra family member.

        Québec applicants follow Québec (MIFI) amounts.

        Before you apply as a family

        1. Choose a programme that keeps your spouse's work-permit option open, if that matters to you.
        2. Budget tuition, rent and living funds for each person — not just yourself.
        3. Decide whether family applies with you or joins later; timing changes the paperwork.
        4. Check school enrolment and health coverage for children in your province.
        Plan my family move →IRCC — Help your family come with you ↗

        General guidance. Family permit rules change often — confirm on canada.ca and at your consultation. Tutee Connect is not a licensed immigration representative.
